Who We Are

Liv Investment (Pty) Ltd is a trusted provider of ICT, Cleaning, and Security solutions based in Midrand, South Africa.

Founded with the mission to deliver top-quality, reliable services, we cater to government institutions, corporate organisations, SMEs, and residential clients. Our team of experienced professionals is dedicated to empowering your business with innovative technology, meticulous cleaning services, and robust security solutions.

We are committed to excellence, integrity, and delivering real value to every client we serve.

Meet Our CEO

Norleen Ramovha

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Norleen Ramovha is the visionary leader behind Liv Investment (Pty) Ltd. With extensive experience in business development, service delivery, and strategic management, she founded the company with a passion for delivering integrated solutions that make a tangible difference.

Under her leadership, Liv Investment has grown into a trusted multi-divisional company serving government institutions, corporates, and communities across South Africa. Norleen is dedicated to empowering her team, building lasting client relationships, and driving innovation in every service division.

Her commitment to excellence, integrity, and transformation continues to guide the company's mission and values every day.
